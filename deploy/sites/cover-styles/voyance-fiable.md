# Style des couvertures — voyance-fiable.com

Observé sur 6 couvertures existantes (`public/images/articles/*-hero.jpeg`, 1376 × 768, 16:9) et la doctrine du dépôt (`skills/voyance-fiable-images/SKILL.md`, `docs/IMAGES-DA.md`).

- **Trois directions selon la rubrique** :
  - tarot, astrologie, numérologie : **gravure dorée** à traits fins (atlas célestes du 17ᵉ siècle, Cellarius, Bayer) sur fond violet nuit uni, beaucoup de vide ;
  - rêves : **collage surréaliste** de papiers découpés et gravures anciennes (Max Ernst), papier vieilli, fond violet profond, feuille d'or sur quelques bords ;
  - voyance (comparatifs, avis), spiritualité : **nature morte photographique** sobre sur velours prune (téléphone ancien, loupe, lettres, livres, balance, réveil), lumière latérale rasante, ombres longues, grain argentique.
- **Palette** : fond `#0f0820` / `#160c2c`, or `#f5c463`, crème `#f6ecd8`, magenta très rare.
- **Lumière** : sombre, une seule source latérale ; jamais de halo ni de lueur.
- **Cadrage** : composition centrée ou asymétrique aérée, vue plongeante ou de trois quarts pour les natures mortes.
- **Interdits** (doctrine du site) : boule de cristal, main mystique, fumée violette, néon, yeux fermés, robe à capuche, pentacle, étoiles simplifiées, œil dans un triangle, pleine lune photoréaliste ; texte, logo, personne reconnaissable.

Fichier produit : `public/images/articles/<date>-<slug>-hero.jpeg`, 1376 × 768, JPEG < 200 Ko. Champs : `cover.src`, `cover.alt`, `images.featured`, `alt.featured`.

## Style commun

```text
Editorial cover image for a sober French divination magazine. Strict palette: deep night purple background #0f0820 / #160c2c, warm gold #f5c463, cream #f6ecd8, matte black; magenta only as a rare tiny accent. Dark, quiet, elegant mood, generous negative space, one single side light source, no glow. NO crystal ball, NO mystical hand pose, NO purple smoke, NO neon glow, NO closed eyes, NO photoreal tarot card with backlight, NO black hooded robe, NO golden pentacle star, NO simplified stars like ★, NO eye-in-triangle, NO photoreal full moon with white halo, NO sparkles.
```

## Variante : tarot

```text
Direction C: engraved illustration in thin gold lines on a flat deep night purple #0f0820 background, style of 17th-19th century astrological engravings modernized as clean line art, no texture, no halftone, no shading fill, centered figure or symbol, wide negative space. No Rider-Waite colour illustration, no roman numerals or lettering.
```

## Variante : astrologie

```text
Direction C: astronomical engraving in thin gold lines on a flat deep night purple #0f0820 background, in the manner of Cellarius' Harmonia Macrocosmica and Bayer's Uranometria, modernized clean linework: orbits, ecliptic arcs, faint constellation lines, planetary glyph inside a thin gold circle. No lettering.
```

## Variante : numerologie

```text
Direction C: numerological engraving in elegant thin gold linework on a flat deep night purple #0f0820 background, faint zodiac wheel arc, alchemical symbols and dotted constellation markers, 18th century treatise style modernized. A single large stylized numeral is allowed only if the article is about that number.
```

## Variante : reves

```text
Direction A: surrealist editorial paper collage in the manner of Max Ernst's 'Une semaine de bonté' and modern paper-cut surrealism: vintage engraving cut-outs, aged paper textures, scattered paper-cut elements, deep purple background, gold leaf catching a few edges, asymmetric dreamlike composition, never kitsch, no horror, no gore.
```

## Variante : voyance

```text
Direction B: editorial still-life photograph in the manner of Wallpaper magazine: two to four sober objects linked to the topic (vintage telephone, magnifying glass, sealed letters, old books, brass scale, alarm clock, smartphone face down) on dark plum velvet, angled or top-down view, dramatic single low side light from the right, long soft shadows, worn gold metal, cream paper, matte blacks, medium-format film aesthetic with slight grain. No glowing or levitating objects.
```

## Variante : spiritualite

```text
Direction B: editorial still-life photograph of one or two concrete objects linked to the topic (stone, unlit candle, bowl of water, clock, notebook) on dark plum velvet, single low side light, long soft shadows, medium-format film grain, sober and calm.
```
